### Curiosity

The hardest part of about being an Autistic (undiagnosed until 42) Christian Conservative growing up was my natural curiosity. I had so many questions. My questions were often answered in one of two ways by pastors, youth pastors, and other Christians.

1. Simplified answers that one should simply take at face value and belief, not allowing doubt to rob them of their assurance. 

2. A call to simply "have faith" and "don't doubt" and an appeal to the mystery of God as if to say "We don't know, we just trust". 

True faith is never blind and does not require a lack of thought. 

Faith (*pistis) is a result of experiences with an individual or instituion that bring about trust. If I say I'll do something but I have a history of flaking, you won't have faith I'll do it. But if I'm known for always doing what I say, you'll have faith I will. That is what faith means. 

We should be curious. We should ask all the questions. There is no question that should not be asked.
